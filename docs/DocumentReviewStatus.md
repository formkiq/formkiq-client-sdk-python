# DocumentReviewStatus

Status of Document Review: - PENDING: Review has been created and is waiting for one or more decisions. - IN_PROGRESS: Review has been claimed or at least one decision has been submitted, but completion requirements are not yet satisfied. - COMPLETED: Completion requirements are satisfied. - CANCELLED: Review was cancelled before completion. - SUPERSEDED: Review was replaced by another review, usually because the artifact/document changed.

## Enum

* `PENDING` (value: `'PENDING'`)

* `IN_PROGRESS` (value: `'IN_PROGRESS'`)

* `COMPLETED` (value: `'COMPLETED'`)

* `CANCELLED` (value: `'CANCELLED'`)

* `SUPERSEDED` (value: `'SUPERSEDED'`)
